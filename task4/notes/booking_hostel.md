# Booking Hostels
The main way to book hostels in Thailand is via websites such as booking.com and Hostelworld.

I found that booking.com is the best way to book hostels and is the cheapest. This is due to Hostelworld adding extra commission.
You then choose the room with the amount of people you are happy sharing a room with. I personally recommend not going above 10 due to people being loud all throughout the night and early in the morning. I found 4 the best as you would be able to get to know the people in your room and have the lowest chance of being kept up all night. Hostels are usually cheaper based on the amount of people in your room e.g. a 12 person room would be much cheaper per night than a 4 bed hostel.
