# Bike rental Thailand


To rent a bike in Thailand you first need to go to a bike rental spot. The price per day depends a lot on the type of bike you are trying to rent.

Small scooter: 120–250 THB per day.
Medium scooter: 200-400 THB per day.
Standard motorbike: 250-500 THB per day.
Large bike: 1000-2000 THB per day.

When renting a bike make sure to take a video of the bike before you use it, making sure all existing scratches and dents are proven to be there before you used it. This is to prevent the common scam of the rental company blaming you for the damage and charging you for it.
