# Street food in prices in Thailand
Street food in Thailand is generally very cheap, especially compared to western prices.

Street food prices tend to change depending on your location and type of food you want to eat.
But on average a standard street food dish costs: 40-70 Thai Baht or around 80p to £1.50.
whereas eating in heavily touristed areas such as Khao San Road street food prices are 2-3x higher.

The price also varies based on what protein you would like with seafood costing the most and tofu usually being the cheapest.
