# Currency

The currency in Thailand is Thai Baht (THB)
The symbol for Baht is: ฿.
1 Thai Baht is equal to 0.022 British Pounds, with 1 Pound being the equivilent of 45 Baht.
The Baht banknotes are as follows: 20 Baht, 50 Baht, 100 Baht, 500 Baht and 1000 Baht.
The Baht coins are as follows: 1 Baht, 2 Baht, 5 Baht and 10 Baht.
