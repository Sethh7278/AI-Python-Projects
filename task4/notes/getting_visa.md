# Visas for British passport holders

As of October 2026, British passport holders can enter Thailand without a visa
for up to 30 days, for tourism only. This changed on 15 September 2026; before
that it was 60 days, so older websites may still say 60.

For a longer stay, apply for a tourist visa through Thailand's e-Visa system
before you travel. It allows a stay of 60 days from arrival.

Every traveller, including visa-free visitors, must complete the Thailand
Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online before arriving.

Visa rules change often, so check the Royal Thai Embassy or the UK government's
Thailand travel advice before booking.

Last checked: October 2026
